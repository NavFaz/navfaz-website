Affiliate disclosure

How Navfaz intends to handle affiliate links transparently.

What an affiliate link means

Some links may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, Navfaz may receive a commission, at no additional cost to you.

Our standards

  • We will not invent affiliate links or claim to be enrolled in a programme before approval.
  • We will identify affiliate links or pages clearly.
  • We will check merchant, product and programme terms before publishing links.
  • We will not claim hands-on testing, ratings or current prices without a sound basis.

Before monetisation

This website starter contains no live affiliate tracking links. Add genuine links only after joining the relevant programme and reviewing its disclosure, branding, traffic and content requirements. The disclosure should be placed close to affiliate links as well as on this page when required.
