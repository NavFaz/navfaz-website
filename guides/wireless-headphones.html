How to choose wireless headphones

Compare comfort, sound, calls, battery and features based on how you will use headphones.

Choose for your main use

Commuting, calls, workouts and focused desk work place different demands on headphones. Start with the situation you care about most rather than choosing by a feature list alone.

Key factors

  • Comfort and fit: Consider clamp force, weight, ear-pad material and whether in-ear tips fit securely.
  • Microphone quality: Look for call samples or independent tests in noisy environments.
  • Noise cancellation: Useful for steady low-frequency noise, but performance varies by model and fit.
  • Battery: Compare real-world results with ANC on and off, not just manufacturer maximums.
  • Controls and compatibility: Check multipoint pairing, device switching, codecs and app requirements.
  • Repairability and warranty: Battery replacement and service support can matter over time.

Prices and availability change. Confirm the exact model, seller, return window and warranty before buying. Navfaz does not claim hands-on testing unless a guide explicitly says so.
