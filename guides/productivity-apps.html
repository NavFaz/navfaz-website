How to evaluate a productivity app

A practical checklist for deciding whether a productivity app is worth using or paying for.

Define the problem first

Choose an app to solve a real recurring problem — such as task capture, team handoffs or note retrieval — rather than adding a tool because it is popular.

Compare the details

  • Free tier limits: Check usage caps, file limits, collaboration and export restrictions.
  • Privacy: Review what data is collected, retention settings, training use and account deletion options.
  • Portability: Make sure you can export your content in a usable format.
  • Integrations: Verify the exact apps and plan level required.
  • Total cost: Include taxes, annual billing, seats and paid add-ons.
  • Accessibility: Check keyboard support, mobile usability and screen-reader compatibility.

Try the free version with a small, realistic workflow before moving important information. Confirm current pricing and terms on the provider's own website.
