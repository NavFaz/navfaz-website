How to choose a laptop for work or study

A practical checklist for comparing laptops without overpaying.

Start with your workload

For documents, web research and video calls, prioritise a comfortable keyboard, a good screen, reliable battery life and enough memory. For software development, creative work or demanding applications, check the recommended specifications for the apps you actually use.

What to compare

  • Memory (RAM): 8 GB can suit basic tasks; 16 GB offers more headroom for multitasking. Check whether it can be upgraded.
  • Storage: An SSD makes everyday use more responsive. Estimate your files and app needs before choosing capacity.
  • Display: Compare resolution, brightness, reflections, size and viewing comfort.
  • Battery and weight: Look for independent testing and consider your real travel pattern.
  • Ports, warranty and service: Confirm the exact model number, included charger, return policy and service options in your area.

Before you buy

Compare the exact configuration rather than relying on a product-family name. Prices, stock and warranty terms change. We do not list a product as the best choice until its current details and purchase destination have been checked.
